Privacy & Consent Notice

How Masana WebWorks collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use the website, request services or communicate with the business.

1. Scope and legal framework

This notice applies when you visit Masana WebWorks online, submit an enquiry, request a quotation, become a client, use support channels, or otherwise provide personal information to Masana WebWorks.

Masana WebWorks aims to process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and other applicable South African law.

2. Responsible party

For the personal information covered by this notice, Masana WebWorks acts as the responsible party unless a project agreement states otherwise.

3. Personal information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with the business, information may include:

  • Name, surname and preferred contact details.
  • Company, organisation or project information.
  • Project requirements, content, preferences and correspondence.
  • Quotation, invoice and payment-related records. Masana WebWorks does not need you to place card credentials into ordinary enquiry forms.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure or maintain a website or digital service you ask Masana WebWorks to support.
  • Website interaction data that is technically generated by hosting or security infrastructure, such as IP address, device/browser information and request logs.
  • Information you voluntarily include in contact forms, email, WhatsApp or other direct communication.
Do not submit highly sensitive information through the general enquiry form unless it is genuinely necessary for the service requested.

4. Why personal information is processed

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotations.
  • To provide web development, digital services, hosting, maintenance, support and related deliverables.
  • To communicate about project scope, progress, approvals, support and account matters.
  • To administer invoices, payments, records and client relationships.
  • To protect systems, investigate misuse and maintain service reliability.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, accounting or contractual obligations.
  • To improve business processes and service quality using information that is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

5. Sources, required information and your choices

Most information is obtained directly from you. Information may also be obtained from a person acting on your behalf, from a business you represent, from public business information, or from technical service providers where this is relevant to providing or securing a service.

General website enquiries are voluntary. However, if information that is necessary to identify the project, communicate with you, issue a quotation or provide a contracted service is not supplied, Masana WebWorks may be unable to proceed with that request.

6. Sharing and service providers

Personal information is not sold. It may be shared only where reasonably necessary with service providers or other recipients involved in delivering the requested service, operating communications, hosting infrastructure, processing payments, maintaining records, obtaining professional advice, or meeting a legal obligation.

Third-party services have their own terms and privacy practices. Masana WebWorks seeks to use appropriate providers and limits shared information to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

7. Cross-border processing

Some hosting, email, cloud, payment, messaging or technology providers may process information outside South Africa. Where cross-border processing is used, Masana WebWorks will seek to apply appropriate safeguards and use providers in a manner consistent with applicable legal requirements.

8. Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for ongoing client support, for legitimate business records, or to comply with applicable legal and contractual requirements. Information that is no longer required should be securely deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of appropriately.

9. Security

Masana WebWorks uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and service. Measures may include controlled access, secure credentials, updates, backups, secure hosting practices and limiting access to those who need it.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a material security compromise involving personal information occurs, Masana WebWorks will respond in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

10. Your rights

Subject to POPIA and applicable limitations, you may ask whether Masana WebWorks holds personal information about you, request access to it, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request deletion where appropriate, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You may also lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator if you believe your personal information has been processed unlawfully.

11. Direct marketing

Masana WebWorks will not treat a project enquiry as unlimited permission to send unrelated marketing. Where consent is required for electronic direct marketing, the business will seek it as required and provide a reasonable way to opt out of future marketing communications.

12. Website assistant and local storage

The current Masana Assistant on this website is a browser-based automated helper. Its chat messages are processed locally in your browser and are not transmitted to Masana WebWorks by the assistant itself.

If you choose a handoff to the website contact form, email or WhatsApp, information you submit through that channel will then be processed for the enquiry. Third-party messaging services such as WhatsApp are also governed by their own privacy terms.

If Masana WebWorks later enables cloud-based AI, analytics or other tracking that materially changes how information is processed, this notice and the relevant consent mechanisms should be updated before that processing is introduced.

13. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when services, systems or legal requirements change. The latest version will be made available on the Masana WebWorks website with an updated effective date.

14. Consent and acknowledgement

Where consent is the appropriate basis for processing, you confirm that you understand the purposes described in this notice and voluntarily consent to the relevant processing. You may withdraw consent in writing, subject to legal, contractual and operational requirements that may continue to justify or require certain processing.
